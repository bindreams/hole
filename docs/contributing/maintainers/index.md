# Maintainers

This section is for the person who releases Hole, and for whoever is on call when a release or an uninstall goes wrong.
It assumes you can already [build and test](../setup.md) Hole.

- [Releasing](releases.md): the four tracks, the draft and publish workflows, and what a draft guarantees.
- [Publishing garter](garter-crates-io.md): the one track that also goes to crates.io, and what to do when that fails.
- [Ship blockers and stated limits](ship-blockers.md): what must stop a `hole` release, and what ships on purpose.
- [Rollback](rollback.md): taking a release back, which only ever moves forward.
- [Rotating the signing key](minisign-key-rotation.md): the order that keeps installed copies updating.
- [When an uninstall refuses to complete](uninstall-gate.md): the Windows gate, forcing past it, and getting the network back.
- [Icons](icons.md): where the icons come from and the macOS tray rule.
